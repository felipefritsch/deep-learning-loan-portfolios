\chapter*{Statement on the Use of Generative AI}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Statement on the Use of Generative AI}

In accordance with the Mathematical Institute's \emph{Guidance for students on
the use of generative artificial intelligence in summatively assessed work} and
the University's Declaration of Authorship, I record below how generative AI
tools were used in preparing this dissertation. I take full responsibility for
the entire content of the submitted work, including any material developed with
the assistance of these tools, and I have checked all such output for
correctness.

\paragraph{Tool.}
The generative AI assistant used was Anthropic's Claude Opus and OpenAI's ChatGPT, 
including one-off command-line coding assistance (Claude Code), used during
May through June 2026.

\paragraph{Software and code.}
The data pipeline---ingestion, cleaning, and construction of the seven-state
monthly transition panel from the Fannie Mae Single-Family Loan Performance
dataset---together with the analysis and modelling code, was developed with
substantial assistance from Claude Code through a \emph{specification-driven,
iterative} process. I wrote the design specifications and task breakdowns; the
assistant generated and revised code one task at a time in side-by-side fashion; and I reviewed each
task against explicit acceptance criteria, ran and debugged the code myself, and
verified its outputs before proceeding. No code was incorporated without my
review or editing at any single point.

\paragraph{Literature.}
Generative AI was used in a limited way to support the literature search. Much of
the technical material here was learned from lecture notes, which do not always
cite their original sources; I used AI to help identify the primary
reference---the textbook or article---from which a given result, algorithm, or
mathematical idea originates. I also used it to scope the mortgage, default, and
mortgage-backed-securities literature, with which I had little prior familiarity
beyond Sirignano, Sadhwani \& Giesecke,% optionally use \citet{<your-bibkey>}
{} the paper that motivated this dissertation. In every case I independently
verified that each reference exists, is relevant, and is correctly attributed,
and all discussion of the sources is my own; no part of the literature review was
written, verbatim or paraphrased, by a generative AI tool.

\paragraph{Writing and interpretation.}
The text of this dissertation---including the introduction, the conclusions, and
all interpretation of the mathematics and of the empirical results---is my own.
Any AI assistance with the prose was limited to spelling and grammar. Generative AI was not used for substantive
original writing or to interpret data or mathematics.

\paragraph{Figures and numerical results.}
All figures were produced by my own (AI-assisted) plotting code from data I
generated. No plot, table, or numerical result was produced by a generative AI
tool, and no data were altered by such a tool much less interpreted by one. 

\paragraph{Data handling.}
The loan-level data is the publicly available Fannie Mae
Single-Family Loan Performance dataset; no confidential or licensed third-party
data were entered into any third-party AI tool.
