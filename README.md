# Deep Learning models of Loan Portfolios and Asset-backed Securities
Research project and dissertation for Oxford University's MSc in Mathematical and Computational Finance.

The commercial and residential loan market is one of the largest asset classes with over \$20 trillion
in value. Banks and financial institutions
hold large loan portfolios and investment funds trade asset-backed securities (ABS) with underlying
pools of small business, mortgage, credit card,
commercial, and student loans. The cashflows from these loan portfolios are a function of the
number of loans which are current, delinquent, prepaid, or
defaulted. Each loan has a high-dimensional set of data features, which can be used to model their
likelihood of becoming delinquent or defaulting. In this dissertation, a deep learning model will be
developed and trained to predict loan delinquency and prepayment. The model will be
benchmarked against simpler statistical models (such as logistic regression). A large dataset of 30+
million loans with billions of monthly data samples will be used to train and evaluate models.
